Chronicle Lift Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 19, 2026

Disclaimer: We've written this policy in plain language to describe what data we collect and how it's used. As Chronicle Lift moves toward public launch, this policy will be reviewed by a legal professional to ensure it fully reflects applicable requirements.

Operator: SVH Personal Training

1. What data we collect

Account information: your email address, and — if you sign in with Google or Apple — the name and email they provide. Profile and training data you enter: workout logs, exercise history, one-rep-max estimates, personal records, readiness check-ins (sleep, mood, energy), training goals and schedule, injury/exclusion preferences, and your onboarding answers. Custom exercises you create, including any photo you attach — photos stay on your device only and are never uploaded. If you turn on Cycle Sync: either dates you enter manually, or — only with your explicit permission — your menstrual cycle dates read from Apple Health. We only read cycle start dates and average cycle length; we never read anything else from Apple Health, and we never write anything back to it.

2. Where your data lives

Most of what's listed above is stored in our cloud database, operated by Supabase and hosted in the EU (Ireland, eu-west-1), so it's available when you sign in on a new device. A small amount of data stays only on your device and is never sent to our servers: an in-progress workout you haven't finished, custom exercise photos, and a few internal app-state flags. We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with third parties for their own purposes — Section 5 lists the service providers who process data strictly to help us run the app, and only for that.

3. Account sync

Earlier versions of Chronicle Lift stored everything only on your device. The app now uses account sign-in and cloud backup, described throughout this policy, so your data follows you across devices.

4. Your control over your data

You can reset training data within the app. Uninstalling removes what's stored on your device, but not your cloud account data. To delete your account and all associated cloud data, email svhpersonaltraining@gmail.com — we'll delete it within 30 days of your request. (A self-service in-app deletion option is planned.)

5. Third-party services

We use a small number of service providers strictly to operate the app; none may use your data for their own purposes such as advertising. Supabase hosts our database and handles sign-in. Google, if you choose "Sign in with Google," authenticates you and shares your name/email with us. Apple, if you choose "Sign in with Apple," does the same; separately, if you enable Cycle Sync, Apple Health on your device is a source we read from with your permission — Apple receives nothing from us through this. Resend sends account emails (such as confirming your email address) on our behalf and only ever sees your email address and that one email's content — never your training data. The app does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs.

6. Children's privacy

Chronicle Lift is not intended for use by children under the age of 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We'll update the effective date above when this policy changes, and note material changes in-app.
