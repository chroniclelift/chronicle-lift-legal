Chronicle Lift Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 13, 2026

This is a placeholder policy for Chronicle Lift's beta phase and has not been reviewed by an attorney. It will be formally revised and reviewed before the app has a general user base or a paid subscription offering.

This policy describes how Chronicle Lift (“the App”), operated by SVH Personal Training, handles your information.

1. What data we collect

The App collects the training and account information you enter directly: workout logs, exercise history, body-composition and readiness data (sleep, mood, energy), one-rep-max estimates, injury/exclusion preferences, and your login credentials (email and a locally-stored password).

2. Where your data lives

As of this version, Chronicle Lift has no backend server. All of the data described above is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to us or to any third party. We do not sell your data and we do not share it with third parties — nothing leaves your device today.

3. Planned changes

We plan to introduce account sync in a future version (currently exploring Supabase for authentication and cloud backup). If we introduce a backend, cloud sync, or any feature that transmits your data off your device, we will update this policy before that feature ships, describing what is collected, why, and how it is protected.

4. Your control over your data

Because your data lives only on your device, you control it directly: you can reset it from within the App, or remove it entirely by uninstalling the App. There is currently no server-side account or data-deletion request process, because there is no server holding your data.

5. Third-party services

The App does not integrate any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting SDKs. For users who opt in to cycle-sync training, the App can optionally read menstrual cycle data from Apple Health under your explicit permission; that data is also stored locally on your device, not transmitted to us.

6. Children's privacy

Chronicle Lift is not intended for use by children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as the App changes. The effective date above reflects the most recent revision.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy or your data can be sent to svhpersonaltraining@icloud.com.
